    Higher depth and MultiPV give more reliable classifications but take longer per move. The "min. gain" threshold is what keeps a forced-but-obvious retreat (e.g. saving a hanging piece) from being scored as a Good move: it must actually improve on where you already stood, not just be the only sane option. A Brilliant is a chain: the first Good move, followed by that many of the player's own subsequent moves all landing Good or Solid, with the chain length scaling smoothly between the two values above based on game phase (whichever of material-left or move-count says it's later). The chain is voided if the opponent played a Mistake or Blunder anywhere in that span — otherwise it would be their error being converted, not a demonstrated combo.
