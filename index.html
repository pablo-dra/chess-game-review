Analysis settings

Higher depth and MultiPV give more reliable classifications but take longer per move. The "min. gain" threshold is what keeps a forced-but-obvious retreat (e.g. saving a hanging piece) from being scored as a Good move: it must actually improve on where you already stood, not just be the only sane option. The two "chain" settings are two independent, additive ways to earn a Brilliant: a scarce-alternatives chain, or a run of playing the engine's actual best move several times while building a real advantage. Both chains also require the opponent to have played reasonably throughout — one of the analyzed candidates, and not one that trailed too far (by the "gap ratio") behind the best ones available to them — otherwise it's their mistake being converted, not your Brilliant.
